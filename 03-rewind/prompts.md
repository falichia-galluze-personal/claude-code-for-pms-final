# 03 · Rewind — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly.

Last session you read four conversations and every support ticket
since 4.2 — and found the two piles did not agree.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.
Can you indicate which responders this happened to? Did this happen to everyone, or did it happen to some people much more than others?

### 2.
Can you add week-by-week rows for the four, and check whether they were pinged first in their own areas before and after?

### 3.
Does this data align or conflict with the tickets raised and feedback given during the interviews? Do the responders overlap between the rook database, customer interviews and support tickets?

### 4.
Can you add week-by-week rows "everyone else" besides the four hardest hit, and check whether they were pinged first in their own areas before and after? Why do we think their experience was different?

### 5.
Can you make a trend graph based on the impact per responder?

### 6.
Can you group this by the 4 responders most impacted vs the other 12?

### 7.
can you also include their taken vs missed % as well with the pings

### 8.
If the Director of Product asked tomorrow what release 4.2 actually did to responders, what's the one number we should put in front of her based on all the information we found from this data?

### 9.
Compare the support_tickets table on rook-database against the pings table. Do they agree with each other? When did people start writing in, and when do the numbers actually move?

### 10.
can you please chart the daily misses against the daily ticket counts in one timeline?

### 11.
Can we dig deeper into where they don't line up? Is there something hidden in the data that was not reflected in the support tickets or interviews? Can we explore what may have happen during that 5 day lag before the ticket volume rose.

### 12.
The previous PM said this was just August being quiet. Is there anything in this file that would tell me whether that was right?

### 13.
Does the other support tickets beside the quiet tickets conflict, match or expose anything in comparison to the ping table, or calls taken or missed?

### 14.
Based on all the information we have gathered thus far from the database and the support tickets. What is a question i should be asking you? And what is the answer?

### 15.
how do we map the people reporting the tickets to the people experiencing the biggest issues from the data?

### 16.
Pick one responder from the file who went quiet, who you think has the most interesting set of data of the quiet four. Show me every week for them, how many times we pinged them, how many they took. Then tell me what happened to that person, week by week, in plain English. Why did you pick them?

### 17.
can you please make that chart? Also is it possible the Gale got all the calls that meteor mite missed? or did they distribute to other responders?

### 18.
Has the gale always been ranked first? before and after the 4.2 release? Have they always responded to more calls compared to Meteor Mite? Did their handler Kip create more tickets for one more than the other?

### 19.
check whether Meteor Mite’s lower pre-4.2 take rate (69%) was already pushing it down the ranking. also can you compare Meteor mite and the gales interview feedback compared to their data?

### 20.
Once somebody's gone quiet, what would have to happen for them to start getting pinged again?

### 21.
Post one sentence in the channel describing what happened to Meteor Mite?

### 22.

### 23.

### 24.
Based on this information, what are some good follow up questions to ask during an interview with Meteor Mite and The Gale? What are some assumption we can make about their experience after the 4.2 release?

### 25.
This theory, "Since 4.2 shipped on 12 Aug, Meteor Mite has gone from about 11 pings a week to 1 (nothing taken since 19 Aug) while The Gale, in the same Eastgate area, went from 13 to 21, and the likeliest cause is the 60s ping wait combined with misses being scored as declines, though that isn’t confirmed."

Walk me through your reasoning, how did you come to the conclusion that the cause is the 60s ping with misses being scored as declines?
