# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

Did anyone say something that nobody else mentioned?

### 2.

Based on the interviews and the feedback given on things they are unhappy about. Can you separate  the themes and request that seem like enhancements rather than defects or things wrong that were introduced in the release?

### 3.

Based on the feedback, how often a theme/item was raised, the type of item (enhancement, long-standing defects, and regression defects from 4.2 release and its severity of impact to the users. Can you create a list of these items and prioritize them?

### 4.

Based on all this information and from our interviews, can you summarize the trouble with the 4.2 release?

### 5.

Who filed these? Give me a list of names and how many of them filed each.

### 6.

Can you indicate if the "starved responders" you indicated before line up with the responder/hero and the quiet tickets? Do most responder either fall in the quiet category OR gone before answering category? Or is there overlap?

### 7.

Is there a theme or a pattern between the responder/handler and the responder/hero in regards to these tickets?

### 8.

Based on the tickets, Is there bias on which handler and responder get picked? Please be considerate of before and after the 4.2 release?

### 9.

Only look at what came in after 12 August. What's different about these compared to everything before?

### 10.

You've now read both folders. Where do they disagree? What's loud in the interviews but rare in the tickets, and what's all over the tickets that nobody brought up in the interviews?

### 11.

If both piles are telling the truth, how can they both be right?

### 12.

How were responders selected for the interview? Why was there only overlap of 1 responder based on tickets filed? Was the interviews held after the tickets were raised? Who do you suggest we interview based on the gaps between the two piles?

### 13.

Are there any contradictions between the interviews and the tickets? If so can you specify what they are?

### 14.

For the responders and handlers you suggested for the next interviews, can you indicate a couple questions and topics to clarify for each that are directed related to them to clarify the gaps and to help figure out what went wrong in the 4.2 release?

### 15.

Based on all this information from the tickets and the interviews, can you finetune our hypothesis on what went wrong in the 4.2 release?

### 16.

If I'd only read the interviews, what would I have got wrong?
